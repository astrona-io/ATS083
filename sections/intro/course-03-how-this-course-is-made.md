# How This Course Is Made

Paris Nakita Kejser at Astrona.io maintains this course. The authors map each section to an official CKNE objective and check each technical statement against the primary documentation of Kubernetes, the CNI specification, flannel and Multus.

The checks are static. Tools such as `astrona validate` and `bash -n` check the configuration and the scripts of each playground and lab. The labs and playgrounds have not been run yet, and the pages do not claim that they have. Technical pages carry a draft mark until review.

The [contributors page](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS083/graphs/contributors) lists the people who maintain the material. To report a mistake, open an issue on the [issues page](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS083/issues) and name the page and the statement. The course uses the [Apache-2.0 license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS083/blob/main/LICENSE).

The objectives belong to The Linux Foundation and CNCF. The teaching text and the knowledge checks are original practice material and are not official exam questions.
